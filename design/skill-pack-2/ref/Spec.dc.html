Skill pack 2 · spec and rulings

Extends design/shell and design/skill-pack. Peeps are the real <peep-bot> from design/peep.js, unchanged. Sizes stay 48 / 96 / 40 / 32; floppies stay one 16 px sprite at 16 / 32 / 48 / 96.

01 Closed shelf

  1. Sits under the last live row, inside the same scroll, behind a striped header: "Closed · last 8h · N" and, when any, "N to check" in accent.
  2. Row: 32 px peep, idle at energy 1, opacity 0.5 dark / 0.55 light, 60% greyscale. Ghost bubble (1 px line outline, muted) with the last bubble text. Name in Plex Mono, hollow end-state chip, time since end.
  3. Third line: ticket stub, then up to four flags in this order: uncommitted, <id> left doing, no Pack Up, ended waiting / ended error (the last one is the chip, not a flag).
  4. Flags: 1 px accent outline, 4 px accent dot, text colour. A flagged row adds a 2 px accent edge to its ghost bubble. No fills, no red, no reorder: newest first always.
  5. Empty: one line, "Nothing ended in the last 8 hours." Collapsed by default when empty.
  6. Over five rows: scroll inside with "N more below · N to check · show all".
  7. Click or Enter copies claude --resume <id>. Reuses Jump: row gets the accent outline, the peep wakes and waves, bubble "On your clipboard!", hint bar with Ctrl+V cap, 6 s.

02 Title pressure

  1. Driven by the higher of 5h and 7d.
  2. < 80: as today. 80: 2 px amber inset on the plate, amber stripes at 40%. 95: plate filled amber, ink text, stripes at 2 px pitch. 100: plate filled red, "· full", 45° hatch.
  3. Crossing 95 plays one 4-frame nudge, 40 ms each (0, −2, +2, 0 px). Nothing loops. Reduced motion: frame 1.

03 Meters with numbers

  1. 10 blocks of 3 px + 1 px gap, then the % in 11 px Plex Mono 600, tabular, min 3ch.
  2. Number and blocks: text colour / accent below 70, amber from 70, red from 90.
  3. Reset time stays in the hover tooltip.

04 Limit bubble

  1. At 80%: every non-attention peep shows sweat. The limit takes the verb slot: "AV-81: rate-limit warning · 5h 82%". With no ticket, the whole line: "5h limit 82%".
  2. An attention state takes the bubble first: the ask, filled, no sweat, no limit text.
  3. Toasts at 95% and 100%, once each per window. Reset time when known, else "Reset time not known yet."

05 Arc line

  1. One 26 px line on the open row between tray and tags: star, north star (ellipsis), "N closed · N open · N parked", tick + last closed id.
  2. Closed count in accent and first, so it is the number that grows. On a close it steps up with a 4-frame pop.
  3. No north star: "No north star yet" muted, then a Jump "The Reel" underlined in the Reel colour.

06 Floppies

  1. Unpack teal, Walk Through lime, The Reel caramel. Each sits apart from the six in hue and lightness in both themes.
  2. Glyphs: Unpack an arrow up out of a box, Walk Through footprints, The Reel the north star.
